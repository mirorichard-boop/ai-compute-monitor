Terms

Token: unit AI output is sold in, about ¾ of a word.

Chip rent: hourly price to use one AI chip in someone else's data center.

A100 → H100 → H200 → B200: Nvidia AI chips, oldest to newest.

Neocloud: company that rents out AI chips, often debt-financed.

Capex: spending on data centers, chips and power gear.

PJM / ERCOT: the Mid-Atlantic (incl. Virginia) and Texas power grids.
